# KB4IT 0.10.0

KB4IT 0.10.0 adds a Markdown editor to every theme, a quality score for techdoc documents and a build version that names the exact commit. Every site is rebuilt once on its first build.

- **Editor:** the Add page edits new documents in a CodeMirror editor that works offline, also from `file://`: completion of property names and values, checks as you type, a Properties form, a toolbar and a Save button. It is on by default in techdoc; blog, bookshelf and apphelp turn it on with `"editor": true` in `repo.json`.
- **Quality score (techdoc):** every document gets a score from 0 to 100 and a tier, with a Quality tab, a Quality page and a Quality guide that says what to add. `"quality": false` turns it off.
- **Document pages (techdoc):** a header strip with badges, the `Summary` under the title, banners for drafts and retired documents, an info card with Details, Quality, Related and History tabs, and better images, code blocks and tables.
- **WorkType (techdoc):** records keep the type of work in `WorkType` (`Change`, `Incident`, `Task`, `Meeting`) and the form of the document in `Category`. Older repositories keep working; `scripts/conv/split_category.py` moves them to the new keys.
- **Build identity:** `kb4it --version` names the commit, and `kb4it doctor` checks an installation. Themes have their own version and fingerprint, shown by `kb4it themes`.
- **Sample apps:** every theme starts you with anonymized sample documents; the techdoc `sapbasis` app now has SAP Basis samples.
- **Faster builds:** unchanged documents are not parsed again and the deploy copies only changed files.

## Before you upgrade

- Every site is rebuilt once on its first build with 0.10.0, and again whenever another build of KB4IT or a changed theme builds it.
- Header values are text, as written. `Bookmark: Yes` now makes the page `Bookmark_Yes.html` instead of `Bookmark_True.html`, and values such as `0.10` or `010` are no longer changed by YAML. Links to the old page names break.
- `kb4it --version` prints two lines. Scripts that read the version should take the first line.
- `kb4it themes` reports `scope=builtin`, `user`, `source` or `theme_path` instead of `global` and `local`.
- Custom themes: a `version` in `theme.json` that is not `X.Y.Z` logs `THEME_VERSION_INVALID`; from 0.11.0 it stops the build.
- Removed APIs deprecated in 0.9: `KB4IT.get_service("Processor")`, `"Compiler"` and `"Deployer"` (use `backend.pipeline`), `set_progress_callback` and `set_compile_start_callback` in `kb4it.services.compiler` (pass the callbacks to `KB4IT(params)`), and `var['repo']['updated']` in templates (use `var['updated']`).
- The bundled themes require KB4IT 0.10.0.
