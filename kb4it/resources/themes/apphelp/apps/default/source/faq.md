# Frequently asked questions

## Where are my documents stored? {#storage}

In the repository folder you picked. My App does not copy them anywhere else.

## Can I use two repositories? {#two-repositories}

Yes. Switch between them with **Open repository**.

## Does My App need internet access? {#internet}

No. Everything works offline.
